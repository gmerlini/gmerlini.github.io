About me
===

My research interests concern modelling and numerical simulation of dynamic phenomena in complex media, with a focus on wave propagation and its application to biomechanics.

Short bio
---
Since April 2025, I have been a postdoctoral researcher at CERMICS (Ecole Nationale des Ponts et Chaussées). Together with Claude Le Bris and Frédéric Legoll, we investigate the development of effective models for the numerical approximation of hyperbolic scalar conservation laws with highly oscillating flux.

Prior to this, I did my PhD at Ecole Polytechnique, under the supervision of Jean-Marc Allain and Sébastien Imperiale. The objective was the development of a mechanical model and numerical tools for the reproduction of shear wave elastographic measurements in the cornea.
