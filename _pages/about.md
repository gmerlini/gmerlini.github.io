Welcome to my homepage! I am a postdoctoral researcher interested in **modelling and numerical simulation** of **wave propagation** and **transport phenomena** in heterogeneous media, with applications to **biomechanics**.

Short bio
---
Since April 2025, I have been working at [CERMICS](https://cermics.enpc.fr) (Ecole Nationale des Ponts et Chaussées) with [Claude Le Bris](https://cermics.gitlab.enpc.fr/lebris/) and [Frédéric Legoll](https://navier-lab.fr/equipe/legoll-frederic/), on effective models for the numerical approximation of hyperbolic scalar conservation laws with highly oscillating flux.

Before that, I did my PhD at Ecole Polytechnique (2025), under the supervision of [Jean-Marc Allain](https://researchportal.ip-paris.fr/fr/persons/jean-marc-allain/) and [Sébastien Imperiale](https://sebastienimperiale.github.io/). I developed a mechanical model and numerical tools to reproduce shear wave elastography measurements in the cornea.

You can download my CV [here](/files/GM_cv_long.pdf).
