About me
===

My research concerns **modelling and numerical simulation** of dynamic phenomena in complex media, with a focus on **wave propagation** and its application to **biomechanics**.

Short bio
---
Since April 2025, I have been a postdoctoral researcher at [CERMICS](https://cermics.enpc.fr) (Ecole Nationale des Ponts et Chaussées). Together with Claude Le Bris and Frédéric Legoll, I work on the development of effective models for the numerical approximation of hyperbolic scalar conservation laws with highly oscillating flux.

Before this, I did my PhD at Ecole Polytechnique, under the supervision of Jean-Marc Allain and Sébastien Imperiale. At that time, I developed a mechanical model and numerical tools to reproduce shear wave elastography measurements in the cornea.
